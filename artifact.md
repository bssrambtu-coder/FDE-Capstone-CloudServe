# Submission document contract

## Retained workbook references

The five source workbooks in `Capstone_Pack/02_Stage_Workbooks` are the design
authority for their completed counterparts. Their navy cover, orange section
numbers and table headers, footer, page geometry, paragraph styles, diagrams,
and instructional text are retained. Only response cells are populated. The
files remain editable DOCX documents.

Stable mapping is by table index, row index, and column index. The template
tables are not recreated, reordered, or restyled. Empty optional rows may stay
empty. Long answers are deliberately concise to avoid changing the source page
flow more than necessary.

## New report and effort record

The report uses the same restrained visual vocabulary: white pages, black
titles and headings, navy table headers, orange accents only inside tables, and
light-gray grid lines. It uses Aptos, 10.5 point body text, numbered headings,
page numbers, repeat table headers, and explicit page breaks at major sections.
The effort record uses the same styles and explicitly separates supplied
historical entries from the reconstructed 25 August to 10 September plan.

## Fidelity and provenance

No source template is overwritten. The supplied dates and hours in
`effort/effort_log.csv` are preserved as unverified self-reported records.
Reconstructed schedule dates are planning dates, not claims about when work was
actually performed. Current evaluations retain their actual 19 September 2026
execution date. The source workbooks contain no external image dependencies.
